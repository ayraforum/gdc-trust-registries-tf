## 3. Review the Governance Framework v0.1 — 20 minutes

Review the [standalone Issuer Trust Registry Governance Framework v0.1](governance-framework/issuer-trust-registry-governance-framework.md), the neutral Task Force draft.

- Does the current government-issuer scope and treatment of authority, listing, and scoped recognition reflect the group's understanding?
- Which open questions need attention first? Candidates include required governance disclosures, basis of listing, status and reason information, privacy and security disclosures, assurance handling, and conformance.
- Do the distinctions between negative, unknown, and unavailable answers, and the treatment of information freshness and point in time, give relying parties enough clarity?
- Agree how comments will be collected, who will maintain the draft, and the review deadline and process for the next version.

Discussion prompts draw on presentation slides 10–11: scoped recognition, how current information must be, and the meaning of negative, unknown, and unavailable answers.

Outcome: Agreed edits, priority unresolved questions, and a review process. Remaining questions can be assigned for follow-up.

## 4. Next pieces of work and coordination — 15 minutes

- Select the next two or three deliverables: for example, resolve priority framework questions, test the draft against concrete registry examples, or refine the shared vocabulary.
- Consider interest and contributors for the future areas named in the presentation: non-government issuers, business registrations, land title registries, education and workforce credentials, and verifier registration. Agree which to explore next and which to defer.
- Identify coordination needed with Track 2 and technical work, particularly on cross-border recognition and the meaning of registry answers.
- Assign an owner, contributors, and a target date to each selected deliverable.

Outcome: A short, prioritized work plan with clear scope and ownership.
